About us & sourcing capabilities

Heritage Jaipur Craftsmanship Meets Modern Export Quality

From our export hub in Jaipur, India, we supply high-quality pure cotton bedsheets to importers, distributors, and hospitality buyers across the USA.

  • Authentic Jaipur Weaves

    Made with 100% pure Indian cotton, sourced close to its textile heritage.

  • End-to-End Quality Inspection

    Fabric testing, thread count verification, and shrinkage control at every stage.

  • Custom Private Labeling (OEM)

    Tailored packaging, custom branding, and product inserts for your program.

  • US Export Compliance

    OEKO-TEX compliant dyes, barcode labeling, and standard-ready packaging.

HG Home Textile sources directly from verified Jaipur master weavers and state-of-the-art processing units. That close, accountable supply network helps us deliver competitive FOB pricing while maintaining consistent quality from fabric selection through final packing.

We bring traditional textile expertise together with dependable production controls, giving US buyers a responsive partner for repeat wholesale, hospitality, and private-label orders.
